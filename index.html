Example Problem: Projectile Range & Max Height

Step 1
Given: Initial velocity ($u$) = $20\text{ m/s}$, Launch angle ($\theta$) = $30^\circ$, $g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$
Step 2
Required: Time of flight ($T$), Max height ($H$), Range ($R$)
Step 3
Formula: $T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$, $H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, $R = \frac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$
Step 4
Substitution: $T = \frac{2 \times 20 \times \sin 30^\circ}{9.8}$
Step 5
Calculation: $T \approx 2.04\text{ s}$, $H \approx 5.10\text{ m}$, $R \approx 35.35\text{ m}$
Step 6
Final Answer: $T = 2.04\text{ s}$, $H = 5.10\text{ m}$, $R = 35.35\text{ m}$
Step 7
Explanation: Horizontal velocity stays constant throughout the trajectory.
